Summarise-a-document feature: check results (2026-11-19)
=========================================================

What it does
- "summarise <document>" / "give me an overview of X" / "tl;dr X" / "what is X about?"
  is matched against the names and titles of the documents in the collection that the
  route searches. One clear match: the document is summarised with no 0.63 gate. It uses
  one whole call if it fits the context budget, otherwise two stages (partial summaries
  of chunk groups in reading order, then one combine). The cap is 12 calls, and the
  answer adds a coverage note if the cap is hit. The citation is that one document.
- Several matches: a numbered "Which one would you like me to summarise?" list.
  No match: the same list of all documents, capped at 10.
- The next message ("the second one", "2", "eval-corpus.txt", "pfizer", "the pdf") is
  resolved against that list. Zero documents gets a plain message.
- Normal questions still go through the normal path.

Code: src/summarise.ts (new), src/rag.ts (summary path at the top of answerQuestion and
answerQuestionStream). server.ts and web/ are unchanged.
